How the signals work

RESEARCH BUY At least 50 closing prices; 20-day average above 50-day average, price above 20-day average, RSI between 45 and 65, and no existing large concentration.

REVIEW RISK Holding exceeds 30% of the portfolio, or technical readings suggest elevated momentum risk.

HOLD / WATCH Other conditions; no clear rule-based entry signal.

NEED PRICE HISTORY Fewer than 50 daily closes; you can still track value, but no directional technical signal is generated.

Daily update setup

To see daily trend signals, the app requires historical prices. Add a free or paid Twelve Data key in Settings, then select “Refresh prices.” Available symbols, data delays and usage limits depend on your provider plan. You can use the app without a key by typing current prices yourself.

Connect market prices

Optional · Twelve Data market-data provider
Get a key at twelvedata.com. Availability, pricing, market coverage and quota are controlled by Twelve Data.

By default your key is kept only in this open tab. Requests go directly from your browser to Twelve Data; no PortfolioPilot server receives the key. Some browsers block API requests from local HTML files — manual price entry always works.

What this version does and does not do

✓ One offline-friendly HTML file · local browser saving · holdings and watchlist · manual price editing · optional API-based daily close history · paper trades · technical screening · age-based uncertainty modeling · CSV and JSON exports.

✕ No broker connection or real orders · no autonomous buying or selling · no push alerts or background refresh when the browser is closed · no forecast guarantee · no tax, corporate-action, dividend or trading-friction accounting.

PortfolioPilot · Personal research and simulation software. Not investment advice. Past results and technical indicators do not ensure future performance.
